Idea 01

Minimal — "2 LEFT" pill, slim countdown bar, 1-col mobile stack.

FOMO · gold pill Countdown · slim bar Mobile · 1-col stack

Replace the AVAILABLE pill with a small filled-gold "2 LEFT" tag. Inset a full-width slim bar between the top row and the bigtime that ticks down toward end-of-day cut-off. On mobile, slabs collapse from a 3-column grid into a 1-column stack with day on the left and time on the right — same information, more thumb-readable.

vs. today: shipped panel hides scarcity entirely. This is the most direct read of your brief — explicit "2 left", explicit countdown, explicit mobile layout — at the cost of a slightly more salesy register.

Idea 02

Editorial — "Two left" italic, serif sentence countdown.

FOMO · serif italic Countdown · written sentence Mobile · compact (no reflow)

Mobius's voice is editorial and advisory. Loud "2 LEFT" tags read as e-commerce. This variant keeps AVAILABLE in the top-right (green flashing dot — Rafał is reachable) and folds both the FOMO and the countdown into a single serif italic sentence below the bigmeta: "Two slots left — Rafał's week closes in 2 h 14 m." Live monospace on the time, serif everywhere else. Same data, NDA-first register.

vs. today: stays in the dossier voice the rest of the program page uses. Lower-conversion than idea 01 but higher-trust. The countdown still ticks live; the prose framing is what changes.

Idea 03

Instrument — capacity rail + flip-clock + horizontal scroll-snap.

FOMO · 12-pip capacity rail Countdown · flip-clock HH:MM:SS Mobile · horizontal scroll-snap

The most "panel-like" treatment. Capacity reads as a 12-pip rail with two pips lit warm-orange — same denominator your shipped panel implies but never shows. Countdown is split into HH / MM / SS flip cards (subtly bordered) so it reads as measurement, not a sales clock. On mobile the slabs overflow horizontally with scroll-snap, so the visitor swipes through days rather than getting a tower.

vs. today: both the FOMO and the countdown look like instrumentation rather than messaging. Best fit if you want the panel to feel like a piece of professional software. Mobile swipe is more delightful than 1-col stack at a small size cost.

Idea 04

Live feed — "4 min ago" ticker + foot countdown + bottom sheet.

FOMO · social-proof ticker Countdown · always-on foot timer Mobile · bottom sheet w/ grabber

Borrows the booking-platform pattern: a thin top-strip shows the most recent booking — "Last reserved 4 min ago · Berlin" — with a blinking warm dot. The countdown lives in a persistent foot rail in the same warm tint, so urgency is always visible without dominating. On mobile the panel becomes a true bottom sheet — top-rounded with a grabber bar — that swipes up over the page content.

vs. today: highest social-proof read of the ten. Carries real conviction risk if you don't actually have a steady booking stream — the feed has to be true. Best where you can wire it to real recent reservations.

Idea 05

Per-slot — tags on each slab, relative time per slot, dense 3-up.

FOMO · per-slab tag Countdown · per-slab relative Mobile · keep 3-up, denser

Push the FOMO and the countdown into the slabs themselves. Each slab gets a tiny corner tag (LAST OPEN / 2 LEFT / FREE) and its own micro relative-time label under the time ("in 18h", "in 1d 19h", "in 2d 21h"). Reduces the need for a separate countdown chip — the timer lives where the decision happens. On mobile the 3-up grid stays 3-up, just at a denser type scale.

vs. today: spreads scarcity across the picker rather than centralising it. Lets visitors compare slot-by-slot urgency at a glance. Fits the panel without adding chrome above or below.

Idea 06

Deadline — "Closes today" headline, stopwatch, sticky CTA.

FOMO · warm-tone deadline framing Countdown · stopwatch row Mobile · sticky CTA at viewport bottom

The most urgent register without going red. Switch the eyebrow to a warm terracotta-tone "CLOSES TODAY · 18:00 CET" and accent the panel border to match. A stopwatch icon row holds the live ticker. On mobile the white CTA detaches and pins to the viewport bottom — the panel scrolls, the "Reserve · 14:00" stays in thumb reach with a subtle gradient mask above.

vs. today: introduces a non-gold accent (terracotta) for the first time on the panel. Stronger conversion signal but dilutes the monochrome-with-gold brand. Use only when the deadline is real (today) and drop back to gold the rest of the week.

Idea 07

Ribbon — corner ribbon, inline meta-line timer, drawer reveal.

FOMO · diagonal "ALMOST FULL" ribbon Countdown · inline in bigmeta Mobile · slides up as drawer

Most decorative variant. A diagonal gold ribbon rotated 35° in the top-right corner reads "ALMOST FULL" — like a press-kit "RESERVED" stamp. The countdown docks inline into the existing bigmeta line with a blinking dot, so no new row is needed. On mobile the panel doesn't sit in the hero — it slides up from the bottom on first scroll, like a drawer.

vs. today: the ribbon adds a small ornament that matches the dossier-stamp register elsewhere on the program page. Lower information density than ideas 03/04 but higher visual confidence.

Idea 08

Pulse — fast-pulsing dot, breathing chip, tighter mobile.

FOMO · pulse-rate signals scarcity Countdown · breathing chip Mobile · drop bigtime to 48 px

Quietest of the ten. Reuse the gold dot you already have, but pulse it faster when 1–2 slots are left so urgency reads as change in motion, not new copy. Add a single rounded chip "Closes Friday · 02:14:38 left" that breathes in luminance. On mobile, the bigtime drops to 48px and the bigmeta to one line so the whole panel fits under the fold.

vs. today: the only addition is one chip and an animation rate change. Lowest-impact polish with measurable conversion lift if the pulse is calibrated. Pairs well with the existing brand register.

Idea 09

Split status — "2 LEFT" / "CLOSES 18:00", per-slab chips, mini bar.

FOMO · explicit pair row Countdown · pair cell + per-slab Mobile · mini bar (collapsed picker)

Make the FOMO and the deadline equal twins by splitting them into a paired row inset between the top header and the bigtime: left cell "2 LEFT THIS WEEK", right cell "CLOSES 18:00 CET". Each slab carries its own micro countdown chip so urgency follows the slot. On mobile the panel collapses fully — just one wide horizontal bar showing "Tue 14:00 · 2 left · Reserve" — that taps to expand the full picker as a sheet.

vs. today: the most information-dense of the ten. Best for visitors who want to compare urgency vs. deadline side-by-side. The mobile "mini bar" is genuinely different — it claims very little vertical space until the visitor commits to picking.

Idea 10

Hairline reveal — animated rule, monospace timer, parity on mobile.

FOMO · "2 LEFT" inline Countdown · animated hairline reveal on mount Mobile · same hairline at narrower scale

Reuse the hairline-rule motif you already have on the program-page hero and on the doc-control row. A single hairline draws in left-to-right on first paint (1.4s ease), revealing an inline strip: "2 LEFT THIS WEEK ────── BOOKINGS CLOSE 02:14:38". The mobile variant uses the same hairline at a slightly tighter type scale — no other reflow, because the rule already works at any width.

vs. today: most architecturally-consistent of the ten — the hairline rule is already a Mobius signature. This idea is the cheapest to build (one new component) and the easiest to roll back (remove the rule). Lowest novelty, highest brand fit.
