Valletta — line-art skyline

Monoline silhouette of Valletta intended as a decorative band on the Malta MEIN program page (between the editorial breadcrumb and the program spine, or as a watermark inside the breadcrumb stage). Drawn in currentColor so the host page tints it — gold on ink for the dossier hero, ink on cream for body sections.

  • Auberge de Castille Classical PM's office — pediment, central portal, flanking windows.
  • St Paul's Pro-Cathedral Anglican, slim Wren-style spire — the narrowest vertical accent.
  • Carmelite Church Ribbed dome on a fluted drum with lantern + cross — the icon.
  • St John's Co-Cathedral Twin bell towers flanking a central pediment and portal.
  • Upper Barrakka wall Crenellated bastion with the Saluting Battery's three cannon.
