Ten ways to redesign the expanded contents disclosure

The toggle (Contents · 9 chapters · +) is fine. The open state is the problem. Today every chapter eats ~95 px, a dotted leader runs the full column, and the nine entries stack to ~850 px — a print-appendix dropped onto the page, decorative rather than navigational. Ten redesigns below, each holding the same nine anchors, the same toggle, and the same brand register; only the surface of the open state changes.

Behavioural contract — held constant across all ten: the toggle stays as Contents · 9 chapters · +. Click any entry → smooth-scroll to that chapter's id + close the disclosure. All nine entries always rendered when open — no overflow, no inner scrolling. Cream spine background, gold accents, italic Cormorant numerals are constants.
I.Compact ledger — tight rows, no leaders II.Phased trio — three groups of three III.Audit doc-control register — tabular monospace IV.Editorial running text — standfirst paragraph V.Stepper journey bar — 9 connected nodes VI.Drop-cap magazine TOC — numerals as drop-caps VII.Marginalia rail — numbered margin notes VIII.Letterpress mosaic — 2×5 hairline grid IX.Running heads with deks — italic taglines X.Pure typography index — narrow column, minimal

Toggle expands to nine ~95 px rows. Each row: italic gold Roman numeral · serif title · dotted leader filling the column · tabular page number. ~850 px total height; the leaders read as filler at any width over ~600 px.

I — Compact ledger

Tight single-line rows, no dotted leaders

The leaders are filler. Strip them out, halve the row padding, replace the page number with a small monospace doc-ref tag, and the nine entries fit in roughly a third of today's height. Every third row drops a darker hairline so the eye still has a rhythm without the leaders doing the work.

vs today — ~850 px → ~310 px. Rows go from 95 px to ~38 px. Loses leaders + page numerals; adds a discreet GV-A…GV-I doc-ref tag that ties to the audit register the rest of the dossier already uses.
Surface
Cream rows with hairline dividers; no card frame; every third hairline reads slightly darker (--hairline-2).
Active
Hover ramps row to gold ink + 8 % gold wash; click → scroll + close.
Mobile
Same layout; numeral column shrinks 36 → 30 px, label drops to 16 px.

II — Phased trio

Three groups of three: Orientation, Substance, Evidence

Nine flat rows pretend every chapter weighs the same. Group them the way the engagement actually moves — Orientation (About, Investment, Benefits) → Substance (Eligibility, Documents, Process) → Evidence (Visa-Free, Case Studies, Licenses) — and suddenly the contents page tells the reader what the dossier is for, not just where to click.

vs today — ~850 px → ~220 px on desktop. Same nine anchors, but framed by three editorial phase headers that double as anchor-able micro-landmarks. Mobile collapses to a single column with the same three headings.
Surface
Three columns separated by hairlines; phase header in italic Cormorant + monospace tag (I–III / IV–VI / VII–IX).
Active
Hover lifts the chapter to gold; phase headers are non-interactive labels, not links.
Mobile
Stacks vertically ≤ 760 px; phase headers stay, hairlines flip from vertical to horizontal.
