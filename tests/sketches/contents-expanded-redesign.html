Three finalists for the expanded contents disclosure

Pruned down from the original ten. The shortlist keeps the three that earn their surface in different registers: a glance-able mosaic, a grouped trio that frames the engagement as a sequence, and a one-row stepper that says "this is a path, not a menu." The seven other drafts (compact ledger, doc-control register, running text, drop-cap TOC, marginalia rail, running heads with deks, pure typography index) are kept in earlier commits on this branch if they need to be revisited.

Behavioural contract — held constant across all three: the toggle stays as Contents · 9 chapters · +. Click any entry → smooth-scroll to that chapter's id + close the disclosure. All nine entries always rendered when open — no overflow, no inner scrolling. Cream spine background, gold accents, italic Cormorant numerals are constants.

Baseline — what ships today

I — Letterpress mosaic

5×2 hairline cells with corner ticks, tenth cell is the colophon

Borrows the page's existing pitch-card corner-tick vocabulary. Nine chapter cells in a 5×2 hairline grid; the tenth cell is deliberately not a chapter — it's a small colophon with edition + imprint. Reads as a printed end-paper, not a list, and uses the asymmetric tenth cell to signal "this is finite, this is what's in the dossier."

vs today — ~850 px → ~250 px on desktop. Loses the row-by-row scan in favour of a glance-able mosaic. Uniform Cormorant typography across the cells; the surface itself does the editorial work via hairlines and corner ticks.
Surface
5 cols × 2 rows; 1 px hairlines; faint gold corner ticks on each cell echo the pitch-card frame; tenth cell carries a subtle gold gradient and a serif imprint line.
Active
Hover wash 8 % gold; corner ticks stay constant. Click → scroll + close. Colophon cell is non-interactive (pointer-events: none).
Mobile
≤ 880 px: 3 cols × 4 rows. ≤ 540 px: 2 cols × 5 rows. Colophon migrates to the trailing position naturally.

II — Phased trio

Three groups of three: Orientation, Substance, Evidence

Nine flat rows pretend every chapter weighs the same. Group them the way the engagement actually moves — Orientation (About, Investment, Benefits) → Substance (Eligibility, Documents, Process) → Evidence (Visa-Free, Case Studies, Licenses) — and suddenly the contents page tells the reader what the dossier is for, not just where to click.

vs today — ~850 px → ~220 px on desktop. Same nine anchors, but framed by three editorial phase headers that double as anchor-able micro-landmarks. Mobile collapses to a single column with the same three headings.
Surface
Three columns separated by hairlines; phase header in italic Cormorant + monospace tag (I–III / IV–VI / VII–IX).
Active
Hover lifts the chapter to gold; phase headers are non-interactive labels, not links.
Mobile
Stacks vertically ≤ 760 px; phase headers stay, hairlines flip from vertical to horizontal.

III — Stepper journey bar

Nine connected nodes in a single horizontal row, journey register

The dossier is a sequence — there's a reason "About" is before "Process". Show that. A single horizontal stepper, nine nodes connected by a hairline, italic Roman numeral inside each node, chapter title beneath. Dense, diagrammatic, and quietly signals "this is a path, not a menu."

vs today — ~850 px → ~150 px (one row). Trades vertical real-estate for horizontal — works because the labels are short. Adds an at-a-glance current-position indicator (filled ink node + gold dot) the existing list can't carry.
Surface
Nine 34 px nodes on a hairline track; italic Cormorant numerals inside; chapter titles below each node, monospace 5/9 progress tag.
Active
Hover lifts the node 2 px, fills it gold; current chapter is rendered as ink-filled with gold dot. Click → scroll + close.
Mobile
Track keeps its 760 px min-width and scrolls horizontally inside the disclosure — preserves the single-row gesture rather than collapsing to a list.
