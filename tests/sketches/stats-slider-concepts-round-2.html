XI · Cover flow
Creative

Cards in perspective — Apple Music's cover flow.

Five cards arranged in 3D depth with the active card at front and centre, two on each side rotated 35° and 45° around Y, scaled and faded with distance. Click any side card to bring it forward; click pips below to jump. Heaviest visual mass of round two — the most "designed".

XII · Magazine spread
Creative

Two cards at a time — like a print spread.

Pairs of cards as facing pages, with a faint hairline gutter down the middle. Paginate by overlapping pairs (I–II, II–III, III–IV, IV–V), so each click advances by one card but two are always visible. Italic Spread I — II indicator top-left and a monospace page reference top-right.

XIII · Split-flap flip
Super creative

Top half flips up, bottom half flips down.

Each card lives in a horizontally split display — top half rotates -90° on X (lifts up and away) while the bottom half rotates from +90° to 0° (drops into place) with a 120 ms stagger. Echoes Vestaboard / airport split-flap signage. Most mechanical of the twenty. Monospace Prev / Next chips.

XV · Hairline bar only
Minimalistic

A 1 px rail with a gold fill. Nothing else.

More restrained than round one's pip rail (V). The strip scrolls normally; the only affordance is a single hairline rail under the cards with a gold fill that traces scroll position. No pips, no arrows, no counter, no fade. The user's gesture is the navigation — the bar just confirms how far they've gone.

XVI · Focal-grow on scroll
Mine #1

The centre card grows. Others stay quiet.

All five cards visible in a scroller; the one nearest the centre of the viewport is at scale 1 and full opacity. Cards either side scale to 0.82 and drop to 0.45 opacity. Continuous transitions on scroll. No buttons — the focal point moves with the gesture.

XVII · Numbered chip bar
Mine #2

Five italic chips: 14+ · 100+ · 32 · 5 · 1.

Above the strip, five italic-serif chips carrying the numerals themselves. Click a chip to jump; active chip is filled gold. Different from V's pips (round 1) because the chips show the value — you know which card you're jumping to before you click. Different from XIV's tabs because chips are pill-shaped and the strip still scrolls below.

XVIII · Slide-up reveal deck
Mine #3

Cards stacked like a Rolodex. Slide the top one up.

Visual deck of five cards stacked with each card peeking a few pixels below the next. The active card sits on top at scale 1; cards behind step down in scale + opacity. Click Next and the top card slides up off-screen, revealing the next. A small italic counter in the corner shows position.

XX · Hover-magnify cells
Mine #5

All five visible. Hover to magnify.

Five cells side-by-side at neutral size; hovering any cell flex-grows it to 3× the space, bumps its numeral from 58 px to 88 px, fades in body copy, and dims the others to 0.45. No buttons, no scroll, no clicks. The mouse is the selector. Touch fallback: tap uses :focus-within.
