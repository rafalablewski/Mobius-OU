Sketch · stats-slider-left-companion

What goes on the left of the focal-grow slider?

Shipped state: focal-grow stats slider, first card centred via padding-inline: calc(50% - card‑w / 2). Side effect: at scrollLeft: 0 the left ~40% of the container is empty air with only the masthead's eyebrow floating in it.

Reframe. Don't shrink the slider — the centred card is correct. Pair it with a left-column companion that makes the empty space say something. Each draft below holds the focal-grow strip on the right and swaps the left for a different treatment: editorial copy, dossier metadata, a jump-nav of all five figures, a decorative anchor, an upgraded audit-trail block. Mocks are static (the slider mechanic is the shipped one); focus is on the left composition.
