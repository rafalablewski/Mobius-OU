Left companion — ten ideas redrawn so they survive the scroll

Ten original drafts (I–X) for the empty zone left of the focal-grow stats slider. v1 of this file placed each companion in the scroller's left padding — which is space that only exists at scrollLeft: 0. The moment the reader slides to card 2, that padding is gone and the companion goes with it. v2 lifts the companion into its own column so it stays put while the slider scrolls beside it.

What changed. Every concept now lives in .section-mock__companion — a 220 px fixed left column, separated from the scroller by a hairline. The scroller (.section-mock__scroller) is its own overflow:auto column to the right. Companion content reacts to the focal stat via data-focal. Each concept is shown in two scroll states side by side: A = focal 14+, B = focal 32.

Where the companion reacts vs stays static:

  1. Reactive (content swaps per focal stat): I standfirst, II pull-quote, III Roman numeral, IV drop-cap, VI audit-trail block, VII mini-TOC, IX colophon, X office portrait.
  2. Stable (single static treatment across all 5 cards): V vertical spine, VIII Malta skyline. Either by design (V is a wordmark; rotating it would defeat the spine metaphor) or because the ornament reads as section identity, not figure-specific commentary.
