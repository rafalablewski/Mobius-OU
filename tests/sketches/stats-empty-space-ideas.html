“The practice in three figures.” — what to do with the empty space

In focal-grow mode the centred stat sits at scale 1; siblings drop to scale 0.86 / opacity 0.4. On wide viewports that leaves a generous block of quiet ivory on the left of the centred card (see screenshot). Ten ideas below — each respecting the brand: editorial, NDA-first, specifics over superlatives, Cormorant italic, gold dot, hairline rules.

What anchors every concept
  1. Companion content sits in the .empty-zone on the left of the focal card. The right ghost is left at opacity 0.4 as today.
  2. Nothing should compete with the numeral. Companion content is quiet: stone / serif / italic.
  3. On change of focal stat, companion content can react (highlight a year, brighten a city, switch a stamp).
  4. All concepts are annotation, not chrome. They explain, contextualise, or quietly invite — never decorate for decoration's sake.

Margin marginalia — a reader's hand on the page

Creative

The left space becomes a marginal note: a hand-written editorial gloss on the centred figure, in a Cormorant-italic substitute (Caveat) tinted gold. A little arrow points across at the numeral. Like a reader who's been through the dossier and pencilled in the question that matters.

Per stat: one short note (≤14 words) and one underline. Rotates with the focal card. Decoration is the editorial argument — nothing else.

03

Practice wall — a serif data terminal, quietly live

Super creative

Treat the left as a private trading desk's wall, but in serif: programme thresholds, ratified deal counts, jurisdiction tax floors. A live-dot pulses; one row updates every ~6 seconds. Numbers are real; deltas are subtle.

This is the most ambitious idea — it claims the visual language of a Bloomberg terminal but renders it as a 19th-century broadsheet. CFA-trained / NYSE-seasoned is no longer a tagline; it's the wall behind the desk.

Cursor on hover — the off-focus cards

Today the off-focus cards (.ht-stats-items at scale 0.86 / opacity 0.4) look like content but behave like background — there's no signal they can be clicked. The minimal change ships alongside whichever concept above we pick: cursor: pointer on non-focal cards, plus a click handler that scrolls them into focus. Same treatment for any clickable companion (year ladder rows, scrubber rows, dotted-map cities).
