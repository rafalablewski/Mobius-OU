“The practice in three figures.” — scroll-proof companion redesigns

Ten concepts for the quiet zone beside the focal-grow stats slider — each rewritten so it survives the slider scrolling. The companion no longer lives in the scroller's left padding (which evaporates the moment card 2 comes into focus); it sits in its own column, stable, while the slider scrolls beside it.

v1 (committed earlier) was wrong about the layout. My first pass placed companion content in the empty ivory zone left of the focal card. That zone is actually the scroller's padding-inline: calc(50% - card-w / 2) — it only exists at scrollLeft: 0. Once the reader slides to card 2 (100+), card 1 (14+) moves left into that zone; on card 3 (32) it's gone entirely. So every v1 mock was lying.

v2 architecture (single change across all 10 concepts):

  1. The section becomes a two-column grid: grid-template-columns: minmax(220px, 280px) 1fr.
  2. Left column holds the companion — stable, doesn't scroll horizontally. Receives position: sticky inside the section so it also stays put if the section is tall.
  3. Right column holds the focal-grow scroller — its own overflow-x: auto, scroll-snap, focal-grow exactly as today.
  4. Companion content can react to the focal stat: a JS class toggle (the same one that adds .is-focal to the active card) also sets data-focal="14"|"100"|"32"|"5"|"1" on the companion. CSS handles the swap.

Each concept below renders two scroll states side by side: State A · focal = 14+ (initial) and State B · focal = 32 (mid-scroll, cards 1–2 clipped off the left edge of the scroller column). The companion holds its position across both — only its content reacts.
