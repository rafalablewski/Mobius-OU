Sketches · BriefPanel — editorial fit

Ten ways to make the calendar feel like the page.

The shipped Tesla touchscreen panel reads as software dropped onto a dossier. The page register around it is editorial — italic Fraunces headlines, monospace doc-control tabular row, gold hairline ornaments and corner-tick frames on the pitch card. These ten sketches retheme the calendar to fit, without changing a line of logic. Same data, same structure, same interactions — different visual register. Each shows desktop and mobile side-by-side; one shared timer drives every live countdown.

Visual onlyNo structure, behaviour or copy changes — only typography, colour, ornament, and surface treatment.
Mobile responsiveEach idea ships a deliberate narrow-width layout (stacked rows, condensed tabular row, smaller serif).
Existing motifs to draw on. The page already uses: monospace doc-control row (top of hero, hairline-bordered key/value cells, "✓ UP TO DATE"); the pitch card with a faint white-on-black gradient and gold corner-tick brackets; italic Fraunces section openers; Newsreader serif for body; gold hairline rules at section breaks. The panel can borrow any of these — the more it reuses, the more it fits.

Idea 01

Cream card invert — the dossier register.

Cream BG Italic Fraunces bigtime Dark CTA slab (inverted)

Invert the entire palette. Cream surface (the same #FAF6EE used in the page's content cards), dark body text, gold accents. The bigtime becomes italic Fraunces 300 — same family as "Freedom of movement". Hairline reveal stays but in gold-on-cream, more visible. CTA flips to a dark slab with cream text. Reads as a private correspondence card pinned to the page rather than a UI panel.

vs. today: the boldest swap of the ten — pure black → cream. Highest brand fit but the most visual departure. The panel stops competing with the dark hero and starts complementing the editorial column on its left.

Idea 02

Doc-control register — audit-style tabular row.

Mirrors page doc-control Monospace throughout Hairline-bordered cells

The most architectural fit. The page already opens with a doc-control row (RBI · DOC PT-GV · LAST REVISION · STATUS ✓ UP TO DATE · CLASS CLIENT). Apply the same register to the booking panel: top tabular strip with category tag + key/value cells (HOST · STATUS · OPEN · CLOSES), hairline-bordered tabular slot grid below, ghost-outline CTA. Reads as another section of the same dossier, not a separate widget.

vs. today: reuses an existing pattern verbatim — cheapest to ship and visually unimpeachable. Loses the dramatic Manrope-200 bigtime; gains absolute brand consistency. The panel stops looking like a vendor widget and starts looking like a row of the document.

Idea 03

Letterpress almanac — full serif on parchment.

Cream parchment BG Cormorant italic numerals Hairline gold double-rules

A printed-almanac register. Subtle warm radial-gradient on cream as parchment ground, two faint gold rules near the top and bottom edges of the panel as if it were a printed page. Bigtime in Cormorant Garamond italic 300 at 88 px — quietly dramatic. Slot times in matching serif italic; days in tabular small caps. CTA is a serif italic line bracketed by em-dashes — no button shape at all. Footer microcopy hairline-tabular.

vs. today: the most editorial of the ten — drops nearly all the "panel" furniture (pill button, hard borders, chunky type) in favour of typesetting. Lowest interactive affordance; highest register match with the page's body copy and section openers.

Idea 04

Pitch-card frame — corner-tick brackets, dossier excerpt.

Reuses .ht-pitch-card surface Gold corner ticks (top-L + bottom-R) Fraunces italic bigtime + slot times

The page's hero already carries a "dossier excerpt" pitch card with a 1 px gold border at 30% opacity, a faint white-on-black gradient, and 14 px corner ticks at top-left and bottom-right. Apply the exact same surface to the booking panel — it stops looking like a separate widget and starts reading as another excerpt of the same dossier. Bigtime + slot times shift to Fraunces italic to match the section headers; CTA becomes a hairline gold ghost button.

vs. today: reuses one of the page's own surface treatments verbatim. Cheapest to ship after idea 02 — the CSS lives in the page already. Visual departure is the corner ticks + serif italic numerals; everything else is restraint.

Idea 05

Magazine sidecar — print sidebar layout.

Cream BG · serif body Drop-cap-style bigtime Dotted-rule slot list

Borrows from print magazine sidecars — a section opener with a thick rule on top, an italic byline, then a tight list with dotted rules between rows. The bigtime functions like a drop cap: italic Cormorant aligned with metadata to the right (day/zone/duration). Slot list is a dotted-rule register where the day reads as small caps and the time as italic numerals. CTA is a confident black slab.

vs. today: the only idea with a "section opener" masthead — a thick black rule above the panel. Reads as the right-rail of a magazine spread next to the program-page article. Best fit when the panel sits hero-right on desktop.

Idea 06

Watermark monogram — large faint M behind content.

Dark BG with gold M watermark Fraunces italic bigtime Hairline gold rule + bracketed timer

Stays dark like the shipped panel but anchors the surface in brand identity. A 280 px Fraunces italic "M" sits behind the content at 6 % opacity, fading off the bottom-right edge — the same Mobius wordmark initial that opens the breadcrumb. Bigtime + slot times shift to Fraunces italic to match the watermark and the page's section headers. Gold border at low opacity replaces the cold white border.

vs. today: least invasive of the dark-on-dark variants — the panel remains a "slab" but with personality. The watermark is a quiet brand cue rather than a UI element, so the panel reads as a piece of branded stationery rather than a SaaS widget.

Idea 07

Wax-seal accent — gold seal in the corner.

Dark slab + corner seal Italic monogram inside the seal Otherwise restrained

Reuses the wax-seal direction explored in the freshness-badge round-two sketches and applies it as a corner ornament for the booking panel. A 56 px circular seal at the top-right with a dashed inner ring, radial gold gradient and an italic Cormorant "M" rotated ~8°. The rest of the panel stays dark and restrained — the seal does the "this is a private document" lift on its own.

vs. today: minimal layout change — adds one decorative element. The seal aligns the booking panel with the sealed-letter visual language Mobius already explored for the freshness badge, so it fits the broader system. Highest decorative payoff for the smallest structural change.

Idea 08

Calling card centred — private correspondence.

Cream calling card · double gold rules Centred typesetting Outline CTA pill

A formal centred layout: cream calling-card surface with double gold rules near the top and bottom edges (mimicking an engraved card), eyebrow + AVAILABLE stacked above an italic byline, a 96 px Cormorant italic bigtime as the centrepiece, dotted-rule slot list, and a hairline-bordered "Reserve" pill button. Reads as a personal invitation, not a booking widget.

vs. today: the most ceremonial of the ten — drops the left-aligned dossier register in favour of centred private- correspondence formatting. Best fit if Mobius wants the panel to feel like a personal invitation rather than a section of the dossier.

Idea 09

Linen letterhead — paper-textured cream.

Linen-textured cream BG Letterhead masthead with reference tag Outline gold CTA

A laid-paper texture (CSS-only — two repeating linear-gradients form a fine cross-weave) on a warm cream ground. Reads as private letterhead. Top of the panel carries a "letterhead masthead" — italic Cormorant wordmark on the left (e.g. Mobius), a monospace reference tag on the right (BP-007/2026) — followed by the eyebrow + AVAILABLE row. Otherwise serif throughout, gold-outline CTA pill, hairline rules everywhere a print designer would put one.

vs. today: distinct from idea 03 (almanac) by virtue of the masthead and the linen weave — this is corporate stationery, where 03 is more like a typeset broadsheet. Best fit if the booking is a piece of formal correspondence between the firm and the family.

Idea 10

Tesla-restrained editorial — minimal change.

Keeps shipped layout Manrope-200 → Fraunces italic 300 Gold border + cream-outline CTA

The most conservative direction — and probably the cheapest to ship. Keep every dimension of the shipped panel exactly as it is, but soften three things: (a) bigtime + slot times shift from cold Manrope-200 to Fraunces italic 300 to match the page's section openers; (b) panel border + the hairline rules around the bigmeta use low-opacity gold instead of the white that currently makes the panel feel like a SaaS modal; (c) CTA loses its solid white fill and becomes a hairline-bordered cream pill that hovers to filled. Nothing else moves.

vs. today: a typographic and chromatic tune-up rather than a redesign. Smallest visual departure of the ten; smallest implementation cost. Worth pairing with idea 04 if the deeper retheme is too much.
