Sketches · Navigation

Between hero and dossier — ten ways to thread nine chapters.

The program page currently jumps from a full-bleed hero straight into the facts grid. Each program needs the same nine-chapter index threaded through it (About · Investment · Benefits · Eligibility · Documents · Process · Visa-Free Countries · Case Studies · Our Licenses), with each chapter occupying its own viewport (1 scroll = 1 tab) — like a 2026 reading deck rather than a long scroll.

Baseline today. Hero ends → .ht-program facts strip begins immediately. No way-finding, no chapter beats, no sense of "where am I in the dossier." All ten ideas below propose the same nine-section spine; they differ only in where the navigation lives, how it announces the active chapter, and how the reader moves between full-screen panels.

Idea I — Roman index card

The dossier opens with a contents page.

Hero ends, the next viewport is a single cream "Contents" card listing all nine chapters in Roman numerals — front-matter of a book. From there, every chapter is its own 100vh panel. A slim chapter band ("II · INVESTMENT") snaps to the top once the reader is past the index.

Scroll
1 wheel-tick = next chapter; CSS scroll-snap-type: y mandatory.
Active
Roman numeral on top band; thin gold underline on TOC entry.
Keyboard
↑ / ↓ steps; 1–9 jumps; Esc returns to index.
Mobile
TOC card stays full-screen; band collapses to "II/IX".

Idea II — Sticky pill rail

A thin pill bar pinned beneath the hero, gold underline travels with the reader.

The most familiar pattern, restrained for the brand: a single 56-px bar sits just below the hero, lists the nine chapters horizontally, and pins to the top of the viewport as soon as the reader leaves the hero. A 1-px gold rule slides under the active chapter. Hairline dividers between pills, no boxes, no chrome.

Scroll
Bar pins on hero exit; chapters scroll-snap; smooth-scroll on click.
Active
Gold 1-px underline animates left/right; label switches from stone to ink.
Keyboard
Tab cycles pills; Enter scrolls; ←/→ arrow within bar.
Mobile
Bar becomes horizontally scroll-snap; left/right edge fade gradients.

Idea III — Left-edge chapter spine

A book spine down the left margin — vertical index, never in the way.

Once the reader is past the hero, a 56-px vertical rail anchors to the left edge of the viewport for the rest of the page. Nine rows, each a Roman numeral and a chapter label rotated 90° (or kept horizontal in expanded mode). A gold dot marks the active chapter. Hover anywhere on the rail expands it to 240 px showing full titles; click commits a snap-scroll to that chapter.

Scroll
Each chapter is its own scroll-snap container; rail position-fixed.
Active
Gold filled dot · row label switches to ink; non-active rows stay stone.
Hover
Rail expands left→right, no layout shift on the chapter slab.
Mobile
Rail collapses to a 44-px floating "II/IX" tab on left edge.

Idea IV — Right-edge dot navigator

Nine silent dots on the right edge — Apple-product-page restraint.

Smallest possible nav: nine 4-px dots stacked vertically and centred on the right edge of the viewport. Active dot fills gold and grows to 6 px; the others are 1-px hairline rings. Hover any dot reveals its label as a small flag pulled leftward. The chapter content has the whole stage; the nav is essentially invisible until called for.

Scroll
Wheel/trackpad snaps one chapter at a time; spacebar pages.
Active
Filled gold dot; ring on the others; tooltip flag on hover only.
Position
position-fixed right:24px, top:50%, translateY(-50%); never on hero.
Mobile
Dots row pin to bottom edge instead of right; tap to jump.

Idea V — Numbered progress rail

A filmstrip across the top — nine ticks, one numeral, one chapter name.

A 36-px hairline rail: nine evenly-spaced ticks across the full width, each labelled 01–09 in monospace. Above the active tick, the chapter name appears in serif italic, drop-anchored. The bar is more progress meter than menu — its job is "where am I in the dossier?" not "let me list every chapter title at once".

Mobile

Idea VI — File-folder paper tabs

Nine manila tabs across the top — a real dossier the reader thumbs through.

Skeuomorphic dossier. Between hero and chapter body, nine paper tabs are fanned across the top of a cream "folder" surface. The active tab is taller, paper-coloured, and has a 1-px gold edge; the inactive tabs sit one notch lower, slightly desaturated, with hairline pencil edges. The chapter content reads as the open page of that folder.

Scroll
Each chapter is a 100vh folder page; turn-page transition (fade + 8px lift).
Active
Active tab = full height + gold edge; siblings sit -6 px lower, paper-shadow.
Hover
Tab rises 4 px and shows full chapter name (truncated to numeral when idle).
Mobile
Tabs become a horizontally swipable rail; only active + neighbours visible.

Idea VII — Big-numeral chapter parker

Each chapter opens like a magazine spread — giant italic numeral, no menu.

The navigation is the typography. Each 100vh chapter opens with a 240-px Cormorant italic numeral pinned to the upper-left, and a tiny "02 / 09" counter in the upper-right. As you scroll, the numeral parks at the top (sticky), the chapter title and body lay out beneath it. No bar, no tabs; the numerals themselves do the way-finding work.

Scroll
scroll-snap one chapter per turn; numeral fades 0→1 as the panel enters.
Active
The numeral itself; counter "02 / 09" glued to top-right corner.
Click
Counter expands on click into a full Roman index overlay (idea I as overlay).
Mobile
Numeral shrinks to 120 px and shifts to top-centre.

Idea VIII — Wax-seal mark + bottom dock

A wax seal opens each chapter; a thin bottom dock carries the controls.

Decorative + functional split. Each chapter opens with the brand wax-seal monogram (reused from the freshness-badge round-two seal), with a Roman numeral set rotated 8° inside it. The actual navigation lives in a slim bottom-fixed dock — ‹ I · About · II · Investment · III · Benefits › — with ‹ and › arrow keys explicitly hinted.

Idea IX — Floating contents drawer

One "Contents · II / IX" pill in the corner — opens a Linear-style overlay.

Quietest of the always-on options. A single small pill floats in the upper-right corner: "Contents · II / IX". Tap it (or press ⌘ K / ?) and a centred glass overlay slides in with the nine chapters listed editorially — Roman numeral, title, a one-line gloss, keyboard hint. Esc closes it. Otherwise the page is silent: chapters scroll-snap with zero chrome.

Scroll
Pure scroll-snap; no peripheral nav consuming pixels.
Trigger

Active
Pill counter "II / IX" updates on intersection-observer; overlay row highlighted.
Mobile
Pill becomes bottom-right floating action button; overlay full-screen.

Idea X — Doc-control register tabs

The nav is just another audit row — reuses the doc-control register verbatim.

The page already speaks in audit-report registers (.ht-breadcrumb__doc-control — CATEGORY · DOC-CODE · LAST REVISED AT). This idea simply adds one more row underneath: CHAPTER, with nine tabular cells I · ABOUT, II · INVT-02, … each cell a hairline-bordered monospace label. The active cell carries a green-dot ON-PAGE tag borrowed from the calendar's AVAILABLE indicator. Zero new design language.

Scroll

Style
JetBrains Mono labels, 0.16em letter-spacing, hairline 1-px gold dividers.
Mobile
Row scrolls horizontally with snap; only 3 cells visible, active centred.
