III.

Merge decisions, pinned

The two patterns each made independent choices in their own files. Where they conflict, this is the resolution.

Decision

  • Marker positionAt end of the lead italic clause, not on the numeral itself. Reads as a sentence-final reference, not as “fourteen-dagger years”. Matches annual-report typography.
  • Marker glyphsKept †, ‡, § as in XX. Roman i / ii / iii would have collided with the Ref. I / II / III tags inside each cell.
  • Marker styleNewsreader italic, gold, 0.7em superscript. Pairs with the Cormorant italic body but stays smaller than the inline numeral.
  • Footnote placementStrip-wide block under all three letters, inside the same letterhead frame. Not per-cell — each cell already carries Ref. tag + sentence + dek + signature; a fourth element would crowd it.
  • Footnote rule0.5 px ink line above the footnote block (mirrors the masthead rule). Dotted rule below it separates the audit stamp.
  • Audit stampBelow the footnotes, not above. Two halves on one line — monospace methodology stamp on the left, gold audit-trail link on the right. Stacks on mobile.
  • Audit URLRenders as href="#" with the visual treatment intact. No route created. Wire it up later when an /audits page exists.
  • Cell signaturesKept per-cell — R.A.. Removing them and signing once at the foot would collapse three letters into one and break the correspondence register.
  • Cell dividers0.5 px dotted vertical between cells (faint), no top/bottom interior rule. Lets the masthead rule and the footnote rule do the framing.
  • Body wrapperCream (--ht-theme-color-5, #FAF6EE) inside hairline-bordered container on cream body (#F4EFE6). Subtle stationery effect without becoming a card.
