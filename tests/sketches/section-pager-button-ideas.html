A button to walk the dossier chapter by chapter

Today the only thing pinned at the foot of the program page is the round "back to top" disc. The reader can scroll, but they can't step — chapter I to II to III without nudging the wheel. Ten ideas for adding a next-chapter (and prev-chapter) control. The shipped chapter spine has nine 100vh sections (I About → IX Licenses); every idea below operates on the same nine anchors, only the surface differs.

Behavioural contract — held constant across all ten: click the control → smooth-scroll to the next chapter's id with scroll-margin-top: var(--ht-spine-band-top) so it lands flush under the page header and chapter band. Reverse for previous. Keyboard mirror: ↓/J next, ↑/K previous. Disabled at chapter I (no prev) and chapter IX (no next), or wraps to first/last in idea 03 only.
I.Stacked dock — ↑/↓ disc pair (replaces back-to-top) II.Right-edge dot rail — nine pips, vertical III.Bottom-centre prev/next pill — labelled neighbours IV.Keyboard-hint disc — ↓ + J/K chord V.Progress dial — filled arc, roman numeral centre VI.Edge tab — pulled from the right margin like a page tab VII.Inline next-chapter ribbon — sticks to chapter foot VIII.Spine band counter as button — no new floating element IX.Hairline film-strip — nine pips along the foot X.Letterpress numeral chip — current Roman + "next" cue

I — Stacked dock

Two discs, one dock ↑ ↓

Cheapest evolution: keep the gold back-to-top disc, stack a second one above it pointing down. The current floating button gains a sibling instead of a replacement. A monospace counter ("II / IX") sits beneath the stack so the reader always knows where they are.

vs today — reuses the existing #back-top position and visual weight; reader gets directional control without learning a new affordance.
Surface
Two 48 px gold discs stacked vertically with 8 px gap, bottom-right of viewport.
Active
↑ disabled at chapter I, ↓ disabled at chapter IX; disabled state drops opacity to 0.35.
Counter
9 px JetBrains Mono "II / IX" pinned under the dock — purely informative, not clickable.
Mobile
Discs shrink to 40 px; counter stays.

↑ + ↓ — pair, not a single disc

II — Right-edge dot rail

Nine pips, vertical

A 1 px gold rule glued to the right edge of the viewport with nine evenly spaced dots — one per chapter. The active dot is filled gold and ringed. Click any dot to jump; a lone ↓ disc near the foot advances by one when the reader doesn't want to choose. Doubles as a constantly-visible progress bar.

vs today — trades the single disc for a thin spine of dots; the reader sees the whole table of contents at all times, not just the next step.
Surface
Vertical hairline 14–22 px from the right edge; nine 7 px pips, active grows to 9 px and rings.
Active
Roman numeral tooltip on hover; current pip pulses on chapter change.
Mobile
Rail collapses to bottom-edge horizontal version (idea IX), or hides; the ↓ disc remains.

progress bar + jump-table in one

III — Bottom-centre pill

Prev · here · next, in a pill

A capsule centred at the foot of the viewport with three cells: "← I · About", italic gold "II · Investment" middle marker, "III · Benefits →". The reader doesn't have to imagine what's next — they read the title before they click. Editorial register. The most information-dense option.

vs today — labelled neighbours instead of an unmarked disc; trades discreetness for clarity. Wider footprint — best when chapter titles are kept short.
Surface
Frosted ink pill, gold hairline border, 9 px caps for prev/next, italic Cormorant for the centre marker.
Active
Hover prev/next reveals chapter title in full; centre cell is non-interactive.
Mobile
Centre marker hides; pill shrinks to ← / → only.

prev · here · next — names, not arrows alone

IV — Keyboard-hint disc

↓ disc with a small J / K caption

A single ↓ disc (and ↑ above it) plus a caption underneath: J next, K prev. Speaks to vim-literate readers without alienating the rest — the disc still works on click. The caption is the lightest possible nudge that the keyboard does the same thing.

vs today — teaches a keyboard binding inside the affordance itself, the way Linear and Superhuman do. Loud-quiet — visible only at rest, fades on scroll.
Surface
Same disc shape as today; J/K chips beneath in JetBrains Mono.
Active
Captions fade out while scrolling, return on idle (200 ms debounce).
Mobile
Captions hidden — the keyboard isn't there.

click works — and so do J / K

V — Progress dial

Dial with a filled arc and a Roman numeral inside

The button itself is the progress indicator. A 60 px disc with a thin gold arc that fills clockwise as the reader advances — at chapter II the arc fills 22 % of the circumference. Click anywhere on the disc to step forward; long-press (or right-click) to step back. Centre carries the italic Roman numeral and "II / IX" caption.

vs today — one element does both jobs (status + action). Calm — no second disc, no rail, no pill. Loses the prev affordance unless you accept long-press.
Surface
SVG ring, 3 px stroke, gold; centre — Cormorant italic numeral over JetBrains Mono caption.
Active
Fill animates on chapter change; click anywhere advances; long-press 400 ms reverses.
Mobile
Same disc, 52 px; long-press still works.

action = status

VI — Edge tab

A page tab pulled from the right margin

A small gold tab fixed to the right edge, hugging it like a paper bookmark. Top half is ↑ (prev), bottom half is ↓ (next), separated by a hairline. Mid-viewport, vertically centred. Reads as "this is a controls dock for paging through the document" the moment the eye lands on it.

vs today — back-to-top disc relocates from corner to mid-edge; freight is similar but the metaphor is "dossier with tabs" rather than "scroll widget".
Surface
32 × 88 px tab, gold fill, ink chevrons, 6 px outer corner radius (left side rounded only).
Active
Hover lifts each half independently; disabled half greys out at chapter I / IX.
Mobile
Tab moves to bottom-right corner (single column ↑ / ↓).

attached to the page, not floating over it

VII — Chapter-foot ribbon

Inline ribbon: "Next — III · Benefits ↓"

No floating widget at all. At the bottom of every chapter section, a full-width ribbon with the next chapter's Roman numeral, italic title and a chevron. While the reader is in chapter II, the ribbon stays pinned to the foot of the viewport (sticky-bottom). When chapter III arrives, the ribbon scrolls into the band that introduces it.

vs today — the affordance lives in the document, not on top of it. Most editorial / least UI-shaped — closest to how a magazine ends a section.
Surface
Top + bottom hairline gold rules, soft gold gradient inside; italic Cormorant title + chevron.
Active
Sticky-bottom for ~80vh of the chapter; inert (no fixed widget) above and below that range.
Mobile
Same ribbon, padding shrinks; chevron stays gold, title stays italic.

no widget — inline + sticky-bottom

VIII — Spine band counter as button

Page the chapter from the band itself

Don't add anything floating. The chapter band ("II · INVESTMENT 02/09") already sits sticky at the top — bolt a tiny ↑/↓ pager onto its right edge, after the counter. Same band, more honest about what it does. Most restrained option; nothing new on screen.

vs today — turns the existing informative band into an interactive one; the back-to-top disc can stay or go independently of this. Zero new floating chrome.
Surface
Pill with two ↑/↓ buttons, hairline gold border, in the spine band's right-edge cluster, after "02 / 09".
Active
Disabled at edges; current chapter numeral updates on scroll, pager sits in same place.
Mobile
"02 / 09" hides; pager stays.

no floater — pager lives in the band

IX — Hairline film-strip

Nine pips along the foot, behind a 1 px gold rule

A thin gold horizontal line glued to the bottom edge of the viewport, with nine evenly spaced pips on it. Active pip enlarges and pops a Cormorant-italic chapter title above it. A small ↓ disc sits above the strip, right-aligned, for the simple "next" gesture. Reads like a film timeline.

vs today — horizontal mirror of idea II; shorter for tall portrait readers, longer for wide ones; pairs naturally with a scroll-snap chapter spine.
Surface
Foot-aligned hairline at bottom: 22 px from edge; pips 7→9 px on active; tooltip rises above.
Active
Tooltip persists for current chapter; hover any pip to peek title.
Mobile
Strip remains; tooltip becomes inline (always shown).

timeline + nudge

X — Letterpress numeral chip

A cream chip with the current Roman and a "next" cue

Bottom-right, a small letterpress-style cream card. Big italic "II" centred, dotted hairline beneath, and small caps "NEXT" with the next chapter's italic name underneath. Click anywhere on the chip to advance. Most decorative of the ten — leans into the dossier feel. Closest cousin to the freshness-badge round-two seal/stamp ideas.

vs today — swaps the gold disc for an editorial card; carries more information at the cost of more pixels. Reads as part of the "audit dossier" register, not a generic UI control.
Surface
78 × ~78 px cream card, gold inset stripe, dotted hairline divider; italic Cormorant 32 px numeral.
Active
Whole chip is the click target; hover lifts -2 px; right-click sends prev (or long-press on touch).
Mobile
Chip 64 px, "next" line truncates on overflow.

numeral + "next" line — dossier register
