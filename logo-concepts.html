Logo Studies · Round One

Mobius & Partners

Ten directions for the wordmark — from refined private-bank classicism to modernist takes on the möbius idea. None are final; they're conversation starters.

01
The Refined Classic

Fraunces wordmark with a wonky italic gold ampersand. Hairline rule and tracked sans tagline. Most directly evolves what's on the site today.

02
The Möbius Mark

A literal möbius ribbon as the icon — geometric, single-line, with a gold "front face" accent. Ties the firm name to a clean, ownable graphic mark.

03
The Stacked Crest

Centered architectural lockup. Mark, name, hairline rules, ampersand and "Partners" stacked on a strict vertical axis. Reads as a heritage advisory plate.

04
The Engraved

Banknote / certificate styling — hairline frame, diamond corner ornaments, light Cormorant capitals and an ornamental ampersand. Old-world, very private bank.

05
The Modern Boutique

Tracked Manrope sans for "MOBIUS" answered by an italic Fraunces "& Partners" below. A two-voice composition — institutional above, personal beneath.

06
The Monogram Lockup

Circular M&P seal anchors a horizontal wordmark. Works as full lockup, or the seal alone scales to favicon, signet, and stationery.

07
The Continental

A single italic flourish for "Möbius" with diaeresis — French/Italian boutique style. Tracked tail beneath for legibility. Personal, signed, distinctly European.

08
The Initial Drop

An illuminated, oversize "M" sets the tone; the rest of the name reads quietly beside it on two lines. Folio / chapter heading energy.

09
The Architectural

Three-line composition: tracked sans names, hairline rules to either side of a single oversized italic ampersand. Quiet, structural, modernist.

10
The Modernist Möbius M

A geometric "M" built from a continuous twisted ribbon — paired with a confident sans wordmark. The most contemporary of the ten, reads well at favicon scale.
