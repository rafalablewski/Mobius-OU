Logo Studies · Round One

Mobius & Partners

Ten directions for the wordmark — from refined private-bank classicism to modernist takes on the möbius idea. None are final; they're conversation starters.

01
The Refined Classic

Fraunces wordmark with a wonky italic gold ampersand. Hairline rule and tracked sans tagline. Most directly evolves what's on the site today.

02
The Möbius Mark

A literal möbius ribbon as the icon — geometric, single-line, with a gold "front face" accent. Ties the firm name to a clean, ownable graphic mark.

03
The Stacked Crest

Centered architectural lockup. Mark, name, hairline rules, ampersand and "Partners" stacked on a strict vertical axis. Reads as a heritage advisory plate.

04
The Engraved

Banknote / certificate styling — hairline frame, diamond corner ornaments, light Cormorant capitals and an ornamental ampersand. Old-world, very private bank.

05
The Modern Boutique

Tracked Manrope sans for "MOBIUS" answered by an italic Fraunces "& Partners" below. A two-voice composition — institutional above, personal beneath.

06
The Monogram Lockup

Circular M&P seal anchors a horizontal wordmark. Works as full lockup, or the seal alone scales to favicon, signet, and stationery.

07
The Continental

A single italic flourish for "Möbius" with diaeresis — French/Italian boutique style. Tracked tail beneath for legibility. Personal, signed, distinctly European.

08
The Initial Drop

An illuminated, oversize "M" sets the tone; the rest of the name reads quietly beside it on two lines. Folio / chapter heading energy.

09
The Architectural

Three-line composition: tracked sans names, hairline rules to either side of a single oversized italic ampersand. Quiet, structural, modernist.

10
The Modernist Möbius M

A geometric "M" built from a continuous twisted ribbon — paired with a confident sans wordmark. The most contemporary of the ten, reads well at favicon scale.

Logo Studies · Round Two

Less, but stranger

Round one stayed inside the private-bank lane. These ten lean into what the firm name actually is: a möbius — continuity, twist, transformation — read through minimalism, futurism, and a more conceptual hand. Some are deliberately uncomfortable.

11
The Infinity Ampersand

The möbius itself becomes the connector — replacing "&". One symbol, two meanings: the firm's name and its philosophy. Hard to copy, easy to remember.

12
The Pure Glyph

A signet, not a wordmark. One custom character — M crossed by a möbius — that stands alone on stationery, app icon, and door plate. The wordmark sits below and shrinks to nothing on second meeting.

13
The Brutalist Block

"M—P" cut out of a single ink-black bar, with a gold rule replacing the ampersand. Architectural, uncompromising, scales from business card to building signage without losing presence.

14
The Coordinate

A wordmark wearing its data. Coordinates of Tallinn (legal home) and Warsaw (operating base), registry number, founding year — futuristic precision wrapped around classical type. Reads like the spec sheet of a sovereign object.

15
The Migration Vector

Two points and a curved trajectory between them. The whole thesis of the firm in a single mark — an origin, a destination, a route plotted with care. Clean, conceptual, reads as both diagram and signature.

16
The Aperture

A six-blade iris cradling a möbius core. Reads as instrument, lens, gateway — the precision of a maker's mark. Futuristic without being garish. Excellent at small sizes; works as a favicon directly.

17
The Passport Seal

A consular stamp — text running the perimeter, möbius mark at the centre, four cardinal points. Works as a wax seal on letters, embossed on covers, stamped on memos. The most direct nod to the firm's actual business.

18
The Continuous Ligature

A custom letterform: M and P drawn as one unbroken stroke, the gold segment marking the möbius turn between them. Ownable, distinctive — closer to a glyph than a typeset wordmark.

19
The Horizon

Almost nothing — a hairline rule broken by a single gold point, Italiana caps beneath. Pure Swiss minimalism. The wordmark is the silence around the dot.

20
The Kinetic Möbius

A single point of gold light traveling continuously around a möbius path. Static everywhere it's printed; alive everywhere it's screen. The brand's first asset that moves — restrained, hypnotic, distinctly contemporary.
