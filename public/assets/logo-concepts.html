The Möbius at three formalities

12, 17 and 20 aren't competing wordmarks — they're the same idea expressed at three different volumes: a private signet, a formal seal, and a digital flagship. Same möbius DNA throughout.

A · Signet № 12

The Glyph

A single character — the M crossed by a clean two-tone möbius. Two S-curves meeting at the centre, no hanging accents. Stands alone on a door plate, business-card corner, favicon.

B · Seal № 17

The Seal

A die-struck official stamp — heavy outer rim, 36 perimeter ticks, formal sans on the top arc, registration field beneath the möbius core. Built to live on letterhead, certificates, NDAs, and embossed covers.

C · Kinetic № 20

The Kinetic

The Glyph's möbius — 1:1, same path data — drawn faintly as a ribbon and traced by a single travelling point of gold light. Static where it's printed, alive where it's screen.

How the System Reads

One möbius geometry — two crossed S-curves, ink and gold — at three densities. Add an M to it and you get the Glyph. Wrap it in a die-struck rim with perimeter text and you get the Seal. Trace it with a single travelling point of light and you get the Kinetic. Favicon, document, homepage are the same decision.
